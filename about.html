Hi, I’m Brendon.

I started Perth Reticulation and Irrigation in 2025 with a simple idea: retic done properly, and gardens looked after by someone who understands the water that keeps them alive.

Retic is my specialty. Most garden problems I see start with water, too much, too little, or in the wrong place, so I fix that first.

I run set rounds in Perth’s north-east and around South Perth, which keeps prices fair and visits on time. Every job finishes with a photo report so you can see exactly what was done.
