Retic first, then turf

Most new lawns fail from uneven watering. We check or install the retic before a single roll goes down.

Turf is laid in individual rolls. Seams are normal and knit together within two to three weeks.

  • Ground levelled and soil improved
  • Retic checked, adjusted or installed
  • Turf laid and rolled
  • Watered in, with aftercare advice
