Questions, answered

The things people ask us most about retic, lawns and gardens.

How do I get a quote?

Text photos and your address to 0421 308 173. Most jobs can be priced the same day without a site visit.

How much does a new retic system cost?

Every yard is different: size, water pressure, and whether you need sprays, rotors or drip. Send photos and we’ll give you a clear quote.

Which days can I water?

Perth has assigned watering days and a winter sprinkler switch-off. Check your days on the Water Corporation website, and we’ll program your controller to match.

Why does my lawn have dry patches when the retic is on?

Usually a blocked or broken head, poor coverage, or water-repellent sandy soil. A station-by-station check finds the cause.

Do you do one-off garden or lawn jobs?

Our regular garden and lawn services are on fixed schedules. One-off clean-ups and larger jobs are quoted separately when we have space.

Are you insured?

Yes. We’re fully insured, and certificates are available on request for strata and commercial clients.
